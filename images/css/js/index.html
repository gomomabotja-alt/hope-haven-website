Who We Are

Founded in 2015, Hope Haven Animal Rescue has saved over 1,000 animals. We are a non-profit organisation dedicated to animal welfare.

Our team of dedicated staff and volunteers work tirelessly to give abandoned and neglected animals a second chance at life.
