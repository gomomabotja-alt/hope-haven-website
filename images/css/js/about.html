About Hope Haven Animal Rescue

Our History

Hope Haven Animal Rescue was founded in 2015 by a group of passionate animal lovers who saw a need for animal rescue services in the community. Over the past decade, we have grown from a small grassroots organisation to a recognised leader in animal welfare. We have rescued over 1,000 animals and facilitated more than 500 adoptions.

Our Mission

To rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome abandoned animals in our community, while promoting animal welfare through education and advocacy.

Our Vision

A world where every animal has a loving home and is treated with compassion and respect.

Our Team
