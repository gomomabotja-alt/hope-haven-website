Our Services

Hope Haven Animal Rescue offers a comprehensive range of services to help animals in need and support the community.

Animal Rescue

We rescue abandoned, neglected, and abused animals. Our team responds to emergency calls and works with local authorities to ensure animals are safe.

  • Emergency animal rescue
  • Animal welfare investigations
  • 24/7 emergency hotline

Rehabilitation

Many animals come to us requiring medical attention and rehabilitation. Our veterinary team provides the care they need to recover and thrive.

  • Veterinary care and treatment
  • Behavioural rehabilitation
  • Spay/neuter services
  • Vaccination programs

Adoption Services

We find loving homes for our animals through our careful adoption process. Our team works with adopters to ensure successful matches.

  • Adoption counselling
  • Post-adoption support
  • Pet education resources

Community Education

We believe education is key to preventing animal cruelty. We offer workshops and resources for the community.

  • School programs
  • Workshops for pet owners
  • Online resources and webinars
